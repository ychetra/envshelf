# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in EnvShelf a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, and any other conduct that would be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers through the private
security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed and
handled confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments,
temporarily or permanently ban contributors, or take any other action they
consider appropriate to protect the community.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
